# Meeting rooms

People register and hold one of five named rooms for a stretch of time.

## Language

**Room**:
One of five meeting rooms named Sirius, Canopus, Vega, Rigel, and Polaris.
_Avoid_: Meeting room, conference room

**User**:
A person registered under one unique username of 3 to 32 English letters, digits, or underscores. Two usernames that differ only by letter case are the same username, and the spelling saved at registration is the one other people see.
_Avoid_: Account, member, admin, email

**Booking**:
A user's hold on one room for 15 minutes up to 8 hours, measured in minutes on the Thailand clock, from an inclusive start until an exclusive end. While it is not cancelled, no other booking may share that room or that user for any of those minutes.
_Avoid_: Reservation, appointment, meeting
