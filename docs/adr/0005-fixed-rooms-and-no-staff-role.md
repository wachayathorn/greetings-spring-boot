# Fixed rooms and no staff role

There is one kind of user. The rooms are Sirius, Canopus, Vega, Rigel, and Polaris, seeded when the process starts, and nothing in the system renames or adds a room. There is no reschedule and no free-slot query. A logged-in user reads bookings and derives a gap from that list.

A staff role that edits the catalog would add a second kind of user and a second authorization path. That stays out on purpose.
